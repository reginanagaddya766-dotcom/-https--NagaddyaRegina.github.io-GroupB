Our journey

History of Glamour Touch

Glamour Touch Unisex Salon was founded in 2002. Since its establishment, the salon has served the beauty and grooming needs of both ladies and gentlemen, with a focus on helping clients look and feel their best.

The salon is located in Ntinda, Kampala, Uganda, on the New Side near the Post Office. Its location makes it a place where customers in Ntinda and the surrounding areas can visit for hair, beauty and grooming services.

This website was created to introduce Glamour Touch to customers online. It allows visitors to learn about the salon's history, explore services, view style inspiration, meet the website project team and make an appointment request.

About Glamour Touch

Glamour Touch Unisex Salon is a beauty and grooming destination for both ladies and gentlemen. We believe that personal style is a way to express confidence and personality.

Our salon aims to provide quality services, a clean environment, friendly customer care and styles that suit each client.
